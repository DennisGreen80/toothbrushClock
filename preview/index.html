Toothbrush timer simulator

  • Tap the clock to start a timer; hold it to open settings
  • Tap a ring to pause/resume (or dismiss when done)
  • Hold a ring to cancel it
  • + adds another timer (up to 4)

Oral-B brushes (simulated BLE)

Switching a brush on starts a toothbrush timer. Off pauses it; it ends if the brush stays off for 30 s. Tapping a toothbrush ring does nothing (the brush button controls it); hold to dismiss.
